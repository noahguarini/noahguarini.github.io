How do I make a GIF?

Open gifit, pick a Live Photo or video, adjust the frame-rate and speed in the live preview, then save it or share it. You can also make one without leaving Messages: in a conversation, tap the apps button and choose gifit.

Where are my GIFs stored?

In an on-device library inside the app. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app removes them, so share or export any you want to keep.

How do I save a GIF someone sent me?

Open the GIF, tap the share button, and choose “Save to gifit.” If you don't see it, tap “More” in the share sheet and turn gifit on (you only do this once).

Why don't I see gifit in Messages?

In a conversation, tap the apps (+) button next to the text field, then scroll the app drawer to find gifit. You can rearrange the drawer to move it up front.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

gifit is designed to work entirely on your device.

Data we collect: none. gifit does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

Photos and videos. When you choose a Live Photo or video, it is used only on your device to create a GIF. The GIFs you make are stored locally on your device (shared only with the app's own Messages and Share extensions on the same device). Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Sharing. When you choose to share or send a GIF, it goes wherever you send it (for example, a Messages conversation) through iOS. That action is entirely under your control.

Children. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
